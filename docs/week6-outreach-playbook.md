## 🎬 Demo video script (60-90 seconds, Android phone)

Record vertical 9:16 on your Android in good light. Use any free editor (CapCut on phone, or just iMovie). Upload to YouTube **unlisted** so links work in emails but it isn't searchable.

### Storyboard

| Time | Visual | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|
| 0–8s | Logo title card → cut to your laptop showing `demo.rekaman.io/oslo-sofa.html` on Chrome | "This is what AR looks like for an Indian e-commerce brand." |
| 8–15s | Cursor scrolls the product page — show "HomeTown" header, armchair photo, ₹49,999 price, badges | "It looks like any normal product page. Until you tap the AR button." |
| 15–25s | Cut to phone POV — your hand holding Android Chrome on the same URL. Tap "Try in your space" | "On a real phone — Android Chrome, no app installed — this loads in three seconds." |
| 25–45s | Camera opens, ground reticle appears, tap to place. Armchair appears in your actual room. Walk around it. | "The shopper now sees the chair at exact real-world scale, in their actual room, while still on the brand's website." |
| 45–60s | Cut to terminal showing the integration code (just the 2 lines: `<script>` + `<div>`). Highlight | "Two lines of code on the brand's site. That's the entire integration. AI generates the 3D model from a single product photo in 90 seconds." |
| 60–75s | Cut back to your face on camera | "Built in Tamil Nadu by one engineer, for Indian e-commerce. ₹3,999 a month for ten products. If you're a brand and tired of returns, email founder@rekaman.io. I'll set you up myself." |
| 75–80s | End card: "rekaman.io · founder@rekaman.io · WhatsApp +91 8248 524449" | (Silent end card) |

### Recording tips

- Record the phone-POV section in your living room with daylight (~10 AM). The armchair AR looks more impressive on real wood floors than tile.
- Hold the phone steady — use a small tripod or prop it against books for the 25-45s walkaround.
- Re-record the voiceover separately if your room has echo. Write the script down first, read off your laptop screen.
- Total post-edit time: 30 minutes. Don't perfectionist this.
- **Save the unlisted YouTube URL as `DEMO_VIDEO_URL`** — paste into every cold email + LinkedIn post.

## 📊 Outreach math (be realistic)

Cold email industry benchmarks for B2B SaaS:
- **30 emails sent → 3-5 opens → 1-2 replies → 0-1 demo call**
- LinkedIn DMs convert ~2× better than cold email if connection accepted

Realistic Week 6 outcome:
- 30 emails + 15 DMs sent → 4-6 replies → 2-3 demo calls booked → 1 paying customer

That's the goal. Don't expect 5 paying customers in Week 6. One paying customer in Week 7 = Plan A on track.

## ⏱ Send schedule (Sunday May 11)

Send emails in two batches for maximum visibility:

- **Sunday 9-11 AM IST**: 15 emails (Tier 1 + 2 brands). Owner inboxes are emptier weekend mornings.
- **Sunday 7-9 PM IST**: 15 emails (Tier 3 + 4 brands). Founders catch up on email Sunday evening before Monday.
- **Monday morning**: 15 LinkedIn DMs sent at 9-10 AM IST (when LinkedIn engagement peaks for Indian audiences).

**Track every send in your spreadsheet. Track every reply.** Reply triage is your Monday/Tuesday morning ritual: 9-10 AM check inbox, reply within 4 hours to anyone who shows interest. Speed wins.

## 🚦 Reply triage protocol

| Reply pattern | Response | Speed |
|---|---|---|
| "Yes, interested in a call" | Send Calendly / 3 specific time slots IST | within 1 hour |
| "Tell me more" / "How does it work?" | Reply with link to demo + PDF one-pager (write Day 4) + offer call | within 4 hours |
| "Pricing seems high" | Don't negotiate by email. Reply: "Happy to walk through ROI on a 20-min call — typical brand recovers cost in first month from returns reduction." | within 4 hours |
| "Send me the SDK docs" | Reply: "Docs are publishing next week. Happy to walk through integration on a 20-min screenshare — much faster than reading docs." Push for a call. | within 4 hours |
| "Not interested / not now" | Reply: "Totally understand. If your priorities change in Q3, my contact is open. Best of luck with {{Brand}}." | within 24 hours |
| "Unsubscribe / stop emailing" | Mark as DO NOT CONTACT in spreadsheet. Don't reply (annoys further). | n/a |
| Bounce / undeliverable | Update spreadsheet, try alternate email pattern OR skip | n/a |
| 5+ days no reply | Send Follow-up template once. Then stop. | day 5-7 |

## 🎁 Demo call structure (20 min)

Once a brand books a call, your goal is "verbal commit to onboard 5 products in next 7 days."

| Time | Phase | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 0-2 min | Intro, mutual context | Build rapport. Ask about their store, returns problem. |
| 2-7 min | Live demo on phone | They open `demo.rekaman.io/oslo-sofa.html` on their own phone. They tap, place, see armchair in their room. Don't show — let THEM do it. |
| 7-12 min | Their products | "Show me your product page. Let's pick 5 you'd want AR on first." Screen-share their site, identify 5. |
| 12-17 min | Pricing + onboarding | "Starter is ₹3,999/mo, includes those 5 products + 500 AR sessions. Can have you live by Friday. Concierge onboarding — I do all the setup, you don't write code." |
| 17-20 min | Close | "Want to start with a 7-day free trial? Send me the 5 product photos via WhatsApp this week, I'll have AR live by next Monday. Sound good?" |

After call: send onboarding YAML template, ask them to fill it (5 minutes of their time), reply within 24 hours.
